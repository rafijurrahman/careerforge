# Anti-AI Human Voice & Linguistic Standards

These binding guidelines are synthesized from peer-reviewed computational linguistics research (*PNAS*, *Science Advances*, *ACL*), the Wikipedia AI Cleanup Project, and leading industry writing guidelines. They apply to all professional application materials, CVs, cover letters, and communications generated by CareerForge.

---

## 1. Zero Excess AI Vocabulary & RLHF Alignment Terms (`WP:AIVOCAB`)
* **Scientific Foundation:** Dr. Dmitry Kobak et al. (*Science Advances*, 2024) proved 300%–1000% excess frequency spikes in post-2022 LLM text. Tom S. Juzek & Zina B. Ward (*ACL*, 2025) proved Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) forces models into synthetic academic formality.
* **Strictly Banned Buzzwords:**
  - *delve, delves, delving, underscore, underscores, underscoring, intricate, meticulously, pivotal, bolster, bolstered, tapestry, landscape, realm, beacon, foster, fostering, cultivate, unrivaled, groundbreaking, paramount, resonate, resonates, seamless, seamlessly, showcase, showcasing, transformative, indelible mark, deeply rooted, cornerstone, multifaceted, dynamic, evolving, leverage, complexities, interplay, burgeoning*.
* **Banned Robotic Transitions:**
  - *furthermore, moreover, additionally, consequently, it is worth noting that, it is important to remember that, in conclusion, to summarize, when it comes to*.
* **Replacement:** Use grounded, active human vocabulary: *important, main, primary, built, wrote, designed, deployed, tested, solved, cut, increased, is, was*.

---

## 2. No Copula Avoidance — Use Direct "Is / Are" (`WP:AINOCOPULA`)
* **Scientific Foundation:** Mingmeng Geng & Roberto Trotta (*SISSA & Imperial College London*, 2024) proved that simple copular verbs (*is*, *are*) dropped over 10% because LLMs artificially inflate sentences using bloated substitutes.
* **Banned Inflations:** Do not write *"serves as a"*, *"marks the"*, *"stands as a"*, *"acts as"*, or *"functions as a"*.
* **Direct Formulation:** Write direct copular verbs: *"Linux is the operating environment"*, never *"Linux serves as the foundational operating platform"*.

---

## 3. Nominal Load Reduction (Active Verbs vs. Abstract Nouns)
* **Scientific Foundation:** Alex Reinhart, Ben Markey, Ronald Yurko et al. (*Carnegie Mellon University & NJIT, PNAS*, 2025) proved LLMs convert active verbs into heavy Latinate abstract nominalizations (`-tion`, `-ment`, `-ance`).
* **Active Verb Enforcement:** Replace abstract noun clusters (*"the implementation of optimization methodologies"*) with direct, active human verbs (*"I optimized database queries"*, *"I refactored the caching layer"*).

---

## 4. No Superficial Present Participle Suffixes (`WP:SUPERFICIAL`)
* **Scientific Foundation:** Reinhart et al. (*PNAS*, 2025) demonstrated that LLMs append participial `-ing` clauses at the end of sentences at rates dramatically exceeding human writing.
* **Banned Patterns:** Never append weak trailing clauses: *", highlighting..."*, *", ensuring..."*, *", reflecting..."*, *", contributing to..."*.
* **Solution:** Use finite active verbs and separate distinct ideas into clean, independent sentences.

---

## 5. Statistical Mechanics: Perplexity (Unpredictability) & Concrete Grounding
* **Avoid Low-Perplexity Canned Clichés:** Avoid predictable corporate essay phrasing (*"combining analytical problem-solving with strategic business impact"*).
* **Maximize Real-World Grounding:** Anchor writing in concrete physical reality, specific tools, real metrics, and candid technical actions (*"profiled memory leaks in the Go backend using pprof"*, *"migrated 45 microservices to Kubernetes clusters"*).

---

## 6. Statistical Mechanics: Burstiness & Rhythmic Variance
* **Break Metronomic Cadence:** AI writes sentences of identical medium length (15–22 words) with uniform clause structures.
* **Enforce High Burstiness:** Blend very short, punchy statements (3–6 words, e.g., *"It worked reliably."*, *"Latency dropped immediately."*) with longer, nuanced technical thoughts.

---

## 7. No Negative Parallelisms (`WP:AIPARALLEL`)
* **Avoid Contrast Clichés:**
  - *"It is not about X, but Y"*
  - *"Not only X, but also Y"*
  - *"Rather than X, I Y"*
* **State Facts Affirmatively:** Directly state what action was taken and what outcome occurred.
